Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

VerifAI ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you access our news analysis platform and services.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you register for an account, we collect your name, email address, and encrypted password credentials. This information is required to authenticate your identity and provide access to authenticated features.

Submitted Content

When you analyze news articles through our interface, we receive the headlines and article text you input. This content is transmitted securely to our machine learning inference engine for processing.

Prediction History

We store a log of analyses performed under your account, including submitted headlines, predictions (Real or Fake), confidence scores, and timestamps. This history is accessible only through your authenticated credentials.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected data solely for the following operational purposes:

  • Authenticating your account and maintaining session security.
  • Executing text preprocessing and machine learning model predictions.
  • Maintaining your private audit log and prediction history.
  • Evaluating aggregated performance metrics and system stability.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or submitted content to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard safeguards to protect data against unauthorized access, modification, disclosure, or destruction. Passwords are salted and hashed using PBKDF2 with SHA-256 before storage. Authentication tokens are issued via cryptographically signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT) with automated expiration.

4. Data Retention

Account credentials and prediction history records are retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of individual records or your complete account history at any time.

5. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information and analysis history linked to your account.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate account details.
  • Request the deletion of your account and associated prediction records.
  • Export your prediction history records for your own records.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us at privacy@verifai.org.
