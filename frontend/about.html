About VerifAI

Overview of our platform, mission, and technology.

What is VerifAI?

VerifAI is a machine learning platform designed to evaluate news articles and estimate their likelihood of being authentic or misleading. By automating text analysis, VerifAI helps readers, students, journalists, and professionals scrutinize information before relying on or distributing it.

The Problem We Address

The volume and velocity of digital content make manual fact-checking difficult. Sensationalism, clickbait, and deliberate misinformation frequently mimic legitimate reporting. VerifAI provides a fast, initial credibility indicator to highlight content that exhibits linguistic and structural characteristics common to fabricated news.

How Machine Learning is Used

Our system employs Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques, including TF-IDF n-gram vectorization and statistical classification algorithms trained on extensive news corpora. The model assesses syntactic structure, vocabulary distribution, and stylistic markers to calculate a probability score for each article.

Important Clarification on Predictions:
VerifAI predictions are statistical estimates generated by algorithmic models. They serve as an assistive tool to guide critical reading and further inquiry—not as definitive determinations of absolute truth or verified factual reality. Users are encouraged to cross-reference multiple primary sources for important decisions.
