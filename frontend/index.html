Methodological Framework

Architected Beyond Black-Box Predictions

VERIFAI dismantles submitted texts into quantitative linguistic metrics, cross-referencing stylistic traits against validated journalistic criteria.

Stylometric & Affective Analysis

Measures lexical entropy, superlative density, punctuation anomalies, and hyperbolic sentiment shifts typical of clickbait manipulation.

Provenance & Named Entity Checks

Scans for journalistic attribution markers, recognized news wire datelines (Reuters, AP), and direct quotations from verifiable entities.

Transparent Token Attribution (XAI)

No opaque predictions. Every scan highlights the specific trigger phrases that contributed to the classification verdict with detailed diagnostic tooltips.

Inference Pipeline

How An Article Is Verified

A deterministic four-phase progression from raw text ingestion to explainable forensic scoring.

STAGE 01

Ingestion & Sanitization

Raw text or target URL is normalized, stripping boilerplate HTML, standardizing typography, and computing lexical density.

STAGE 02

Feature Vectorization

Extraction of TF-IDF n-grams, readability scores (Flesch-Kincaid), and dependency parse trees to isolate sensationalist tropes.

STAGE 03

Ensemble Classification

Neural classification weights calibrated against ISOT & WELFake corpora determine classification probability thresholds.

STAGE 04

XAI Token Attribution

Token-level importance weights are projected back onto the original text for transparent review by journalists and researchers.
